The Real Problem

The Lead Leak Happens
After the Click

You're already paying for ads, SEO, and referrals. But most businesses lose their best opportunities not at the top of the funnel — but in the critical minutes after a lead makes contact.

Minute 0–2

Form Submitted — No Response

The lead fills out your form. They're hot. Your inbox doesn't notify anyone. No auto-reply arrives. They wait.

Minute 2–15

Call Goes to Voicemail

They call. You're on a job. The phone rings out. No voicemail transcription, no call-back reminder. The moment passes.

Hour 1–4

Message Sits Unread

The Facebook or Instagram DM sits unseen. The lead is still warm — but that window is closing fast with every passing hour.

Day 1–3

Inconsistent Follow-Up

You remember to follow up — sometimes. Other times the lead gets added to a mental list that's never revisited.

Day 3–7

No Qualification System

There's no system to filter serious buyers from tire-kickers. Your calendar fills with wrong-fit appointments — or not at all.

Lost

They Booked a Competitor

The lead didn't ghost you — they went to whoever called back first. Speed wins. The competitor had a system. You didn't.
